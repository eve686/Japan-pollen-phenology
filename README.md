# Artificial Light at Night and Pollen Phenology in Japan
This repository accompanies a research study investigating the effects of artificial light at night (ALAN), climate, and urbanization on pollen phenology in Japan.

The study focuses on two major allergenic tree species:

- Japanese cedar (*Cryptomeria japonica*)
- Japanese cypress (*Chamaecyparis obtusa*)

The analyses examine spatial and temporal variation in pollen phenology and pollen abundance using long-term monitoring records and environmental datasets.
